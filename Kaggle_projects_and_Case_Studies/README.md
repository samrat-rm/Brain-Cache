## 💼 Business Case Studies
*Real business datasets from Scaler's applied ML program — focused on actionable recommendations, not just model accuracy.*

### [OLA Driver Churn — Ensemble Methods](Business_case_study/OLA_Drivers_Ensemble.ipynb)
Predicting driver attrition for a ride-hailing platform.
- Decision Trees with **Bagging & Boosting** (compared and tuned)
- Deep EDA on driver demographics, income, and performance ratings
- Delivered **business recommendations** for driver retention strategy

### [LoanTap Credit Underwriting — Logistic Regression](Business_case_study/LoanTap_Logistic_Regression.ipynb)
Should this loan be approved? Credit-risk modeling for a lending fintech.
- Multi-model comparison with precision/recall trade-off analysis
- Feature engineering on borrower financials
- Recommendations balancing **default risk vs. business growth**
